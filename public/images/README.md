# Images

Final approved website images will go in the nested folders under `public/images/`.

- `team/` for approved portraits and team photos
- `backgrounds/` for approved hero, texture, mountain, forest, bridge, and paper motif assets
- `icons/` for approved supporting icon artwork
- `brand-world/` for approved public homepage visual-system assets promoted after Max's June 4, 2026 publish request
- `studio-preview-next/` for private `/studio-preview-9k4m2x` owner-review assets from the next aesthetic pass; keep these out of public routes until Max approves promotion

Keep raw Google Photos exports, private references, EXIF/GPS metadata, and unapproved personal images out of `public/`.
